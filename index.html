Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

At NiyuktiPoints, we prioritize the security and privacy of Diploma Engineering students and exam aspirants. This Privacy Policy details how your data is handled when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google OAuth Authentication: We use "Sign in with Google" exclusively. When you sign in, we receive your verified email address and basic profile name via Google OAuth to create your student profile. We never see or store your passwords.
  • Academic Activity: We store your community doubts, resource submissions, and reward coins inside our Row-Level-Secured (RLS) Supabase database.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential browser storage solely to keep you signed in across sessions, remember your Dark/Light theme preference, and save your cookie consent choice. We do not sell personal data to third-party advertisers.

3. Data Security

All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS (TLS). Database tables are protected by PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring users can only modify their own permitted records.

4. Contact Us

For account deletion requests or privacy inquiries, email us at kp.team.support@gmail.com.
